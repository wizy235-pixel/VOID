How VOID treats your notes.

Your notes belong to you.

VOID is designed as your personal writing space. Your notes are used to provide the features of the app, such as writing, editing, searching and organizing your content.

VOID does not use your private notes for advertising or sell your note content to advertisers.

Your notes remain under your control. You can export your data using Backup & Restore.
